# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Project spaces

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the [@rzyns GitHub profile](https://github.com/rzyns). Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive personal information.

Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith with attention to context, safety, and proportionality.
